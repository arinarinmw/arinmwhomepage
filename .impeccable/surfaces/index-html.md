# Surface brief: homepage (index.html)

## Scope and mode

Persuade. Replacement visual world for the homepage only; free.html, decode-work.html and the two download pages keep site.css and their current look. The homepage gets its own stylesheet (home.css).

## Audience, job, action, proof, constraints

- Visitor: an event organizer (HR, L&D, OD, university) who got the link from a colleague, often on a phone in LINE.
- Job: decide in a minute whether to invite Arin, and for which session.
- Action: email Arin about the event (mailto, with the four-point brief).
- Real proof on hand: 3 YouTube highlights, 4 campus-visit photos, 3 course covers, 7 roles, 8 certificates, education.
- Constraints: content, bilingual EN/TH toggle (English default), Thai name never translated, Thai titles as published, static HTML/CSS/JS only, no invented numbers, testimonials, logos or counters.

## Chosen direction and memorable moment

Minihompy Room (chosen by the user from the decision round). The homepage is Arin's profile as one tiny isometric room; every object is a section and a real piece of proof. Memorable moment: the room settles into place, and clicking any object in it jumps to that section.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The homepage is Arin's profile as one tiny isometric room where every object is a section and a piece of real proof, so an organizer walks the whole offer without meeting a template. It refuses the headshot-plus-Book-me speaker page.

OWN-WORLD: Pastel isometric room: pink wallpaper, sky wall, butter-and-sky tile floor, sticker furnishings with soft indigo outlines. Lavender page ground, white panels with a soft ledge, indigo ink, one violet action colour. Mitr rounded labels over Sarabun text. Compact rounded label chips on every object.

STORY: Within seconds the organizer sees who Arin is, sees that the room holds real videos, photos, courses and credentials, and finds one violet button that emails her about their event.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left, profile header, the tagline as the headline, and the violet Email button under it. Centre, the whole room at about 620px with a chip on each object and a room-chip bar beneath it. Right, three small panels: mood (her tagline), what is in this room (true counts only), leave a note (email). The room stays whole at every width while panels restack around it.
